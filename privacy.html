Privacy Policy

Live Location Lookup respects your privacy. This site does not require an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not store your location.

The live location tool uses your browser's geolocation API. Your coordinates are processed in your browser and displayed to you only. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party by this site.

This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics that identify you. Standard web server logs, if kept by the hosting provider, may record your IP address and browser type for security purposes, as is normal for any website.

If you contact us by email, we will use your email address only to reply to you. We do not share it with anyone else.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@livelocationlookup.github.io.
